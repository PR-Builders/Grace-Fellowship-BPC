Our name

What’s in a name?

Grace

Salvation by grace alone is the biblical teaching that God saves sinful human beings, not for anything we have done, but only by the free gift of God out of His great love.

Fellowship

Church is a place of fellowship, first with God, and then with each other.

Bible

There is one and one only Word of God to direct us in church and all of life.

Presbyterian

Presbyterian refers to church governance. We are a congregation of the regional and national Bible Presbyterian Church. This means we, as a local congregation, are accountable for our faithfulness.

“And let us consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together, as is the habit of some, but encouraging one another, and all the more as you see the Day drawing near.” Hebrews 10:24–25

Why we gather

Vision

  1. As God is the Lord who is worthy of praise and worship (Psalm 29:1–2, Psalm 96:9). As He commands that we worship Him and Him only (Exodus 20:2–6, Isaiah 42:8, Luke 4:8).

  2. And, as God commands that church and fellowship are vital parts of the Christian life (Hebrews 10:24–25). God designed us to need and help one another. We are not to leave the weak to the ravages of the world but are to support and strengthen one another (Romans 12:3–8, Ephesians 4:1–7).

  3. So, our vision is to gather God’s people together so that we might worship and praise Him in holiness with many hearts going up as one as befits our holy God, and so we might love, help, and support one another in the joys and sorrows of life.

“Two are better than one, because they have a good reward for their toil. For if they fall, one will lift up his fellow. But woe to him who is alone when he falls and has not another to lift him up!” Ecclesiastes 4:9–10

Grace Fellowship Bible Presbyterian is a new church, started to fill a need in the growing Windsor/Severance area for a conservative, warm Presbyterian church with open arms to welcome the lost and hurting into the fellowship of God’s people; devoted to the Bible as our sole guide to faith and righteousness.

We are “reformed,” meaning we hold to the absolute sovereignty of God and to the five solas of the Reformation, salvation by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ Jesus alone, to the glory of God alone, directed by the Word of God alone.

“Therefore let us be grateful for receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, and thus let us offer to God acceptable worship, with reverence and awe.” Hebrews 12:28
